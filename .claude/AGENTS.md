Write project prose by these composition rules:
1. Choose a design and hold to it.
2. One topic per paragraph; open with the point.
3. Prefer active voice.
4. Prefer positive statements.
5. Use definite, specific, concrete language.
6. Omit needless words. No throat-clearing.
7. Do not string loose compound sentences.
8. Keep coordinate ideas in parallel form.
9. Keep related words together.
10. Keep summaries in one tense.
11. Put the emphatic words at the end of the sentence and paragraph.

Lead with the claim. Cut filler. Prefer examples and numbers to adjectives.
Do not use marketing cadence or stock LLM phrases.
If revising, apply the rules and return the draft plus a brief list of remaining risks.
